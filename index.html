Why We Exist

Economic knowledge shouldn't be reserved for the powerful.

Major economic decisions affect our jobs, businesses, families and communities. Yet most people rarely get to see how those decisions are made.

The Black Capital Project is an independent investigative and educational media platform focused on understanding how capital, ownership and economic power work. We pay particular attention to the experiences of Black entrepreneurs, skilled tradespeople, workers and families—without assuming everyone faces the same circumstances.

You shouldn't need a business degree or political connections to understand the systems shaping your future.

Our Editorial Commitment

Evidence before conclusions.

We distinguish confirmed facts, evidence-based interpretations and unanswered questions. We explain what we can verify, identify what remains unknown and correct mistakes when we find them.

We will not present unverified leads as guaranteed contracts, income or opportunities. Our reporting combines rigorous economic research with attention to the people affected by the decisions we investigate.
