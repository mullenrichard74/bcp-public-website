Why We Exist

Economic intelligence should be accessible.

Important financial and business decisions affect workers, skilled tradespeople, entrepreneurs, and families every day. Yet the information needed to understand those decisions is often difficult to find, interpret, or act upon.

BCP connects rigorous research with the real human experiences behind economic headlines. We aim to explain not only where money moves, but what those movements could mean for people and their futures.

Our Editorial Standard

Evidence before conclusion.

BCP distinguishes confirmed facts, reasonable inferences, and unanswered questions. We do not present tentative leads as guaranteed contracts, earnings, or opportunities.

Our goal is practical, trustworthy intelligence that respects the challenges and aspirations of the people we serve.
